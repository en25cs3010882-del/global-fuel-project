3 Subsidy vs price

Average petrol price (USD/L) by subsidy level.
  • Oceania taxes fuel most (avg 38.7%) and South America least (18.2%).
  • Heavily subsidised fuel sells for $0.15/L on average versus $3.45/L with low subsidy.
  • Across countries, subsidy and tax move apart (correlation -0.85); subsidy and petrol price correlate at -0.87.

6 Volatility heatmap

Petrol price variation (std / mean) by region and year. Darker is less stable.
  • Average diesel rose from $1.39/L in 2020 to $2.47/L in 2026; the trend line projects $2.75/L by 2027-04.

Scenario 3: Energy economics research

Prof. Elena Rodriguez studies how income relates to tax, subsidy and price.

7 Tax by income

Average tax %.

8 Subsidy by income

Average subsidy score (1 low to 4 very high).

9 Price by income

Average price, USD/L.
  • High-income countries tax fuel at 34.7% against 22.9% for low-income ones, and pay $3.55/L versus $1.39/L.
  • 88% of high-income records have low subsidy, while 35% of low-income records have high or very high subsidy.
  • Middle-income countries tax least (21.1%), so tax alone does not rise with income.
Subsidy level in the dataset is a category (Low, Medium, High, Very High), scored 1 to 4 here. Tax is the dataset's tax percentage.
